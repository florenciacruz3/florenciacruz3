## About Me

I am a technical writer with a background in English and Cinema and Media Studies from the University of Washington. I spent years guiding people through complex decisions quickly, clearly, and without frustration, first in customer service, now in documentation.

My focus is translating complex subjects into clear, structured content that works for the reader it was written for. I specialize in accessibility, plain language, and information architecture, making sure content is not just technically accurate but genuinely usable by the people who need it.

Right now, I am a Technical Writer & Onboarding Storyteller Intern at Empressa® | AI Women Trust and an Account Executive Intern at Informed Decision. I have built this portfolio under the mentorship of a Microsoft MVP in Data & AI Platforms.

I am open to roles and collaborations on:

- Accessibility audits and evaluation reports
- Plain language rewrites and content redesign
- Technical how-to guides and onboarding documentation
- UX writing and content design
- Product video scripts and onboarding stories
- Project documentation: charters, sprint plans and status reporting
- Editing and proofreading on existing documentation
